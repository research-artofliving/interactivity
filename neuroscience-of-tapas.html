Conceptual illustration on a schematic brain, not a literal image of neural activity or rewiring. These regions are not a 'tapas detector': the same areas take part in many choices, wise or not. Bars, highlights and line weight show direction of change only, not measured values.

What was simplified. Region shapes, positions and link paths are illustrative. The lateral prefrontal cortex and insula lie on the outer surface of the brain and are shown projected onto this midline view. Links between regions are drawn as direct arrows; real connections are networks and loops, and the striatum's influence on the cortex runs through loops via the thalamus.
