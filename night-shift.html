The Night Shift

The impact of Sudarshan Kriya Yoga (SKY) practice on sleep

Every night, we slip out of consciousness for several hours and wake up restored. While we are away, the brain revisits the day, strengthening some memories and letting others fade. That work follows a pattern.

Stages of sleep
A healthy night moves through cycles of about 90 minutes.

What the SKY studies found

↓ 20%insomnia scores in physicians over eight weeksKorkmaz et al., 2024
↓ 31%daytime sleepiness in young adults after eight weeksChaudhari et al., 2020
77% vs 60%daily vs occasional practitioners who sleep wellSloan & Kanchibhotla, 2020

Sleep architecture, measured overnight

Both studies recorded time spent in each stage of sleep: one in a sleep laboratory, the other with a contactless bedside sensor.

Two kinds of rest.

Sleep is nature's built-in reset; it asks nothing of us. SKY is a deliberate breathing practice that leaves one in a quiet, meditative state, and the research suggests the rest we cultivate by day may deepen the rest that comes at night.
