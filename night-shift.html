The Night Shift

From restless nights to restful sleep: what research shows about Sudarshan Kriya Yoga (SKY) and the shape of a night.

Every night, we slip out of consciousness for several hours, and wake up restored.

Sleep is nature's built-in reset. While we are away, the brain revisits the day, strengthens some memories, lets others fade and links the new to the old. That work follows a pattern, and how the hours are spent matters as much as how many there are.
